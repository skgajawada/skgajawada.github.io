About the Course

Engineering Mechanics provides the fundamental principles required to understand the behavior of engineering systems under the action of forces and motion. The course develops a systematic approach to force analysis, equilibrium, friction, geometric properties of areas, structural systems and particle and rigid-body dynamics. Through mathematical derivations, free-body diagrams, worked examples and interactive visualizations, students can connect physical phenomena with their mathematical representation.
