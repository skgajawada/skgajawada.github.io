Hypocycloid — Study Notes

Definition: A hypocycloid is the curve generated by a fixed point on the circumference of a circle that rolls without slipping on the inside of a directing circle.

Generation of a Hypocycloid

In the rolling-circle method, a generating circle rolls internally inside a directing circle. A selected point on the circumference of the generating circle is traced as the generating circle moves through the required angular interval.

Important Terminology

TermMeaning
Directing circleThe fixed circle on whose inside the generating circle rolls.
Generating circleThe smaller circle that rolls internally without slipping.
Generating pointThe fixed point on the generating circle whose locus forms the hypocycloid.
Centre of directing circleThe fixed centre about which the generating-circle centre moves.
Division pointsAngular positions used to divide the required arc into equal parts for construction.

Basic Construction Principle

  1. Draw the directing circle and mark the required starting and ending positions.
  2. Divide the required angular arc into equal parts.
  3. Locate the successive centres of the generating circle on the corresponding radial positions.
  4. Draw the generating circle at each retained centre.
  5. Transfer the equal divisions of the generating circle as it rolls without slipping.
  6. Locate the corresponding generating points and join them with a smooth curve.

Step-by-Step Construction

  1. Draw the directing circle. Draw the fixed directing circle with centre O.
  2. Mark the required angular interval. Identify the starting and ending radial positions.
  3. Divide the arc. Divide the specified angular interval into 12 equal parts.
  4. Locate successive centres. Since the generating circle rolls internally, its centre remains at a distance R − r from O.
  5. Draw the generating-circle positions. At each centre, retain the generating-circle construction as required.
  6. Transfer the divisions. Carry the material divisions around the generating circle according to the rolling condition.
  7. Locate the generated points. Mark the corresponding points P0, P1, …, P12.
  8. Draw the locus. Join the generated points with a smooth curve to obtain the hypocycloid.
For internal rolling, the centre of the generating circle moves on a circle of radius R − r about the directing-circle centre.

Learning Objectives

Understand the generation of a hypocycloid by internal rolling.
Identify the directing circle, generating circle and generating point.
Divide the required angular interval into equal parts.
Locate successive centres of the rolling generating circle.
Transfer the generating-circle divisions while maintaining the rolling condition.
Construct and interpret the final hypocycloidal locus.

Practice & Worked-Example Guidance

Practice 1: A generating circle rolls inside a directing circle. Draw the first three successive positions of the generating circle and identify the corresponding generated points.
Practice 2: Divide a specified angular interval into 12 equal parts and construct the successive centre positions of the generating circle.
Practice 3: Explain why the centre of the generating circle moves on a circle of radius R − r during internal rolling.
Practice 4: Using the interactive animation, pause at each division and identify the current centre, contact point and generated point.
